Electrical Calculation Formulas & Power Integration

• Motor DC Power: P_motor = 12V × I_motor(A) (Rated 0.3A · 3.6W)

• Dual Heaters Power: P_heater = 12V × I_heater(A) (Max 80W / 6.67A)

• Servo Valve Power: P_servo = 6.0V × 0.70A = 4.2W (When Valve Active)

• Total Instantaneous Power: P_total = P_motor + P_heater + P_servo

• Cumulative Energy (Wh): Energy = ∫ P_total · dt

• Kilowatt-hours (kWh): Energy (kWh) = Energy (Wh) / 1000
